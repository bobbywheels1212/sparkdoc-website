What We Do

The electrical work we actually do

Panel upgrades, replacements, and repairs. Permits pulled. Same-day when we can.

Panel Upgrade

Outdated or overloaded panel? We handle safe, code-compliant upgrades that protect your home and support modern electrical loads.

Panel Replacement

Failed, unsafe, or obsolete breaker box? We replace Federal Pacific, Zinsco, and damaged panels. Same amps, or a 100A to 200A upgrade if the house needs it.

EV Charger

Charge at home with a Level 2 charger. We run the dedicated circuit, pull the permit, and install it.

Rewiring

From new construction wiring to complete rewires of older homes, we bring your system up to modern safety standards.

Emergency Electrician

Sparking panel, burning smell, or the house went dark. Call to ask; we help with urgent electrical problems when available.

GFCI Outlet Repair

GFCI trips, will not reset, or needs install in kitchens, baths, garages, and outdoors. We diagnose failed devices vs. wiring and fix it.

Dedicated Circuits

High-demand appliances need their own circuit. We install dedicated circuits for home offices, hot tubs, HVAC, and more.

Surge Protection

One power surge can destroy thousands in electronics. A whole-home surge protector safeguards everything in your home.

Electrical Inspection

Buying a home or worried about aging wiring? We inspect the panel and circuits and tell you what actually needs work.

Troubleshooting

Flickering lights, tripped breakers, a room that went dead. We find the fault and fix it, same day when we can.

Common problems we diagnose: flickering lights, tripped breakers, dead outlets, partial power, sparking panel, and burning smell at the panel. Start with electrical troubleshooting.
