Burning Smell or Sparks
at Your Breaker Panel?

What a Burning Smell Usually Means

Electrical burning is not the same as a dusty heater smell that fades after a few minutes. Persistent odor at the panel points to heat on a connection that should stay cool.

  • Loose lug or breaker that arcs and heats insulation
  • Overloaded circuit cooking a breaker that will not clear the fault cleanly
  • Failing main or branch breaker with worn contacts
  • Damaged or recalled panel (including Federal Pacific and Zinsco) that needs replacement, not another reset

What Causes a Panel to Spark?

This is a fire hazard

Electrical arcing inside a panel generates temperatures exceeding 35,000°F, hotter than the surface of the sun. It can ignite the wooden framing around your panel in seconds. If you see smoke or flames, leave the house and call 911 immediately.

Sparking happens when electricity jumps (arcs) across a gap between two conductive surfaces that should be in solid contact. In a breaker panel, the most common causes are:

  • Loose wire lugs: Wires connecting to breakers or bus bars that have loosened over time, allowing arcing at the connection point.
  • Worn or failing breakers: Breakers that no longer snap firmly into the bus bar lose proper contact and can arc.
  • Moisture or corrosion: Water intrusion or condensation creates conductive paths between components that shouldn't touch.
  • Recalled panel brands: Federal Pacific (Stab-Lok) and Zinsco panels are known to fail to trip under overload and are prone to arcing. If you have one of these, consider replacement regardless of symptoms.

Buzzing or Crackling at the Panel

Buzzing or crackling from the panel is the sound of electrical arcing, electricity jumping across a loose or failing connection. It is the same process that creates visible sparks, just at a level you may not see yet. Both the sound and visible sparks should be treated as emergencies: stop resetting breakers and call (913) 390-3780.

Repair or Replace?

Not every burning or sparking panel needs full replacement. A single failing breaker or loose lug can be repaired. But if the panel itself shows signs of heat damage, the bus bars are corroded or scorched, or you have a recalled brand like Federal Pacific or Zinsco, replacement is the safer long-term choice. That work is covered on our panel upgrade and replacement page. We'll give you an honest assessment and upfront pricing, no upselling.

What Happens on the Visit

We treat panel odor and arcing as a heat hunt: make the panel safe to inspect, find the hot spot, and tell you whether a breaker swap, lug repair, or full panel replacement is the honest fix.

  1. Secure the scene. Confirm there is no active smoke or fire risk before opening the cover.
  2. Inspect for heat and arcing. Look at breakers, bus bars, and lugs for discoloration, scorch, melt, or loose connections.
  3. Test and isolate. Identify which circuit or the main is involved and meter under controlled conditions.
  4. Quote the real fix. Repair when it is safe and lasting. Recommend a panel replacement when the panel is damaged or recalled (including Federal Pacific and Zinsco).

Common Questions

Burning Smell or Sparks FAQ

Is a burning smell or sparking at the panel dangerous?
Yes. Either one usually means a connection or breaker is overheating or arcing. That heat can melt wiring insulation, ignite surrounding materials, and start a fire inside the panel or wall. Call a licensed electrician instead of waiting to see if it goes away.
What should I do right now?
Stop resetting breakers. Do not touch the panel, and keep people away from it. If you see smoke or flames, leave and call 911. If there is no smoke, call Spark Doc Electric at (913) 390-3780 and describe what you smell, hear, or see and when it started.
What does buzzing or crackling mean?
Buzzing or crackling is the sound of electrical arcing, electricity jumping across a loose or failing connection. It is the same process that creates visible sparks, just at a level that may not be visible yet. Both sounds and visible sparks should be treated as emergencies.
Can it be dust?
Sometimes a rarely used heater or motor smells briefly when dust burns off. A smell that comes from the breaker panel, returns, or pairs with heat, trips, sparks, or flicker is not dust. Get it checked.
Repair or full replacement?
It depends on the cause. A single failing breaker or loose lug can often be repaired. If the bus is damaged, the panel is scorched, or the brand is Federal Pacific or Zinsco, replacement is usually the right call. We diagnose first and quote honestly.
What causes a panel to spark?
Common causes: loose wire connections or bus bar lugs that allow electricity to arc; a failing or worn breaker that no longer makes solid contact; corrosion or moisture inside the panel; or an overloaded circuit causing heat buildup and eventual arcing.
