Zinsco Panels: Risks and Replacement

Zinsco panels, including many badged Sylvania or GTE-Sylvania, are an older design with a known heat problem at the breaker bus. This guide is only about that family of panels. If yours says Federal Pacific or Stab-Lok, use the Stab-Lok page.

Quick answer

If the label says Zinsco, Sylvania-Zinsco, or GTE-Sylvania, plan on a panel replacement. The known problem is the aluminum bus and breakers that can overheat and fail to trip. Federal Pacific Stab-Lok is a different panel.

How to recognize one

Zinsco panels were installed from the 1960s into the 1970s, and the design continued under Sylvania and GTE-Sylvania labels after the company changed hands. In a Johnson County house of that age, including older Olathe neighborhoods, it is worth reading the label. Colored breaker handles, often blue, green, or red, are a clue. The words on the label are the confirmation.

Look with the door open and the metal cover still on. If you see melted plastic, browning at a breaker, or a breaker that will not seat firmly, stop using that circuit and call. Those are signs the connection at the bus is already in trouble. They are not a homeowner repair.

What goes wrong

The bus bar is aluminum. The breakers clip onto it. Those clips can loosen with age and heat. A loose clip sparks and heats. The breaker can weld itself to the bus. A welded breaker may not trip, which is the opposite of protection, and it can be difficult to remove without damaging the bus further.

This is why a handful of new breakers is not the repair. The bus is part of the failure. Replacing the panel removes the bus, the breakers, and the guesswork. If the house needs more capacity at the same time, we combine the replacement with an upgrade. If the service size is already right, we change the panel and land the existing circuits.

Pushmatic and Challenger are other older brands people mix up with these. We identify them on site. They are not Zinsco, and they are not Federal Pacific. The Stab-Lok guide is the page for Federal Pacific.

Replacement, permits, and insurance letters

A panel change needs a permit. In Olathe the inspection is through the City of Olathe. We pull the permit, schedule the outage for a daytime window, label the new panel, and meet the inspector. Most residential change-outs are measured in hours, not days, when the scope is a straight replacement.

If an insurer or a buyer's inspector flagged the panel, bring the note. Some carriers require replacement. We will not tell you that every carrier does. We will tell you what the equipment is, and we will replace it if that is the recommendation, which it is for Zinsco.

Common Questions

Zinsco panel questions

Are Zinsco panels unsafe?
Zinsco panels, including many later labeled Sylvania or GTE-Sylvania, are associated with breakers that overheat at the bus and with breakers that can fail to trip. The usual recommendation is to replace the panel. That is a planning conversation, not a claim that your panel is on fire today.
What is the actual failure?
These panels use an aluminum bus. The breaker clips can loosen. A loose connection makes heat. Heat can weld a breaker to the bus, and a breaker that is welded in place may not trip. You can also see discoloration or melted plastic at the breaker. Any of that is a reason to stop using the panel and call, not a reason to keep resetting breakers.
Is a Sylvania panel the same thing?
Many panels labeled Sylvania-Zinsco or GTE-Sylvania are the Zinsco design under a later brand. The label is what matters. Federal Pacific Stab-Lok is a different company and a different panel. If the cover says Federal Pacific or Stab-Lok, use the Stab-Lok guide instead.
Can new breakers fix a Zinsco panel?
No. Breakers for that bus do not correct the bus design. Replacement of the panel is the recommendation. If you are still on 100 amps and you are adding load, the replacement can also be an upgrade.
Will insurance make me replace it?
Some insurers flag Zinsco panels at purchase, renewal, or after an inspection. Not every company treats them the same way, and we do not speak for your carrier. If a letter arrived, bring it to the assessment and we will replace the panel under a permit.
