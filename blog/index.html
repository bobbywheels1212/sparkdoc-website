Burning Smell or Sparks at Your Breaker Panel?

Sparks, buzzing, crackling, or a burning smell at the breaker panel means stop using that panel and call a licensed electrician. Do not keep resetting breakers.

Federal Pacific or Zinsco Panel in Your Home?

If your panel says Federal Pacific, FPE, Stab-Lok, Zinsco, or Sylvania Zinsco, plan on replacement rather than breaker swaps.
