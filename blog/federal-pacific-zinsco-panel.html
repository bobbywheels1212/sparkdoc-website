If your panel says Federal Pacific, FPE, Stab-Lok, Zinsco, or Sylvania Zinsco, plan on replacement rather than breaker swaps. We confirm the brand on site, quote a same-amperage replacement or a 100A to 200A upgrade if the house needs capacity, then handle permit and inspection. Call (913) 390-3780 or book online.

Why These Panels Get Replaced

Federal Pacific Stab-Lok and Zinsco designs from mid-century housing stock show higher rates of breakers that fail to clear a fault. That is the opposite of what a breaker is for.

  • Breakers that may not trip under overload or short
  • Insurance flags at sale, refinance, or renewal
  • No safe path to modern AFCI/GFCI and EV loads on a failing platform
  • Heat, odor, or nuisance issues that point at the can, not one circuit
How to spot the label

Open the panel door (not the dead front). Look for Federal Pacific, FPE, Stab-Lok, Zinsco, or GTE-Sylvania branding on the cover or breakers. Take a photo and text (913) 390-3780 if you are unsure.

Replacement vs. Upgrade

If the house already has enough amperage, we replace the unsafe can at the same size. If you are still on 100A and adding HVAC, a shop, or an EV charger, we often move you to 200A in the same project. Details on our panel upgrade and replacement page.

Olathe and most Kansas City metro cities require a permit and inspection for panel change-outs. We pull the permit, schedule the inspection, and leave the panel labeled and ready.

Common Questions

Federal Pacific & Zinsco FAQ

Are Federal Pacific Stab-Lok panels dangerous?
They have a long record of breakers that may fail to trip under overload or short-circuit conditions. That is why insurers and home inspectors commonly recommend full replacement rather than continued use.
Are Zinsco panels unsafe?
Zinsco and some Sylvania Zinsco panels are associated with breakers that can fail to clear faults and with bus connection problems. Replacement is the standard recommendation.
Can you just replace the breakers instead of the whole panel?
Compatible modern breakers are not a reliable long-term fix for these platforms. The safe answer is a new panel. We will show you why on site rather than sell a temporary workaround.
Do I need a permit to replace a Federal Pacific or Zinsco panel in Olathe?
Yes in Olathe and in most Kansas City metro jurisdictions. Spark Doc Electric pulls the permit and coordinates inspection as part of the job.
How long does replacement take and will I lose power?
Most residential change-outs are a planned daytime outage measured in hours, not days. We schedule the window with you, land every circuit, and restore power the same visit whenever the scope allows.
