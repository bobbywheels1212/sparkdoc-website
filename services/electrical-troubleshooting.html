How Spark Doc Diagnoses a Problem

Guessing is how you replace the wrong part and still have a dead kitchen. We isolate the circuit, test the connections, and find the fault, loose neutral, failed breaker, GFCI upstream, damaged splice, overloaded bus, then we fix that.

  • Walk the symptoms with you, when it started, what was running, what you already tried
  • Check the panel first: tripped breakers, heat, odor, loose bus, double-taps
  • Meter the circuit, voltage, continuity, GFCI/AFCI behavior, not just “reset and hope”
  • Open the likely devices: dead outlets, switches, junctions, and the last-working receptacle on a daisy chain
  • Repair on the spot when the fix is clear; quote honestly if the panel or wiring is the real issue

If the box is the problem, not a single circuit, we will say so. That may be a panel replacement or a 100A to 200A panel upgrade, not another breaker swap.

What to Do Before We Arrive

A few notes from you save time on site. Do not keep resetting a breaker that trips immediately. Do not ignore a burning smell or visible sparks, that is an emergency, not a DIY afternoon.

  • Note which rooms or appliances went out, and whether it is one circuit or half the house
  • Check GFCI buttons in baths, garage, kitchen, and outdoors. One tripped GFCI can kill a string of outlets. See GFCI outlet repair if RESET will not stick
  • If a breaker will not stay on, leave it off and call. Forcing it is how panels get damaged
  • Smoke, flames, or a hot panel: get out and call 911, then Spark Doc at (913) 390-3780

True emergencies, sparking panel, burning smell, total power loss, exposed live parts, go to our 24/7 emergency electrician page. We are available around the clock for those.

Common Questions

Troubleshooting FAQ

What happens on an electrical troubleshooting visit?
We confirm the symptoms, inspect the panel, meter the affected circuits, and open devices only where the tests point. You get a clear cause and a price before we repair. Licensed work in Olathe, Johnson County, and the Kansas City metro, backed by a 5-year warranty.
Can you usually fix it the same day?
Most single-circuit repairs, dead outlets, a bad breaker, a tripped GFCI chain, a loose splice, are same-day. If the panel itself is failed or undersized, we schedule the replacement or 100A to 200A upgrade instead of a band-aid.
Should I keep resetting a breaker that trips?
Once is fine. If it trips again, leave it off and call (913) 390-3780. Repeat resets on a short or a failing breaker is how you get heat at the bus and a much bigger repair.
When is an electrical problem an emergency?
Sparks or flames, a burning smell, a panel that is hot or buzzing, total power loss, or water in the electrical equipment. Do not wait for a booked slot. Use the 24/7 emergency line. Flicker in one fixture or a single dead outlet can usually wait for the next appointment.
Do I need a diagnostic if I already know it is the panel?
If the box is sparking, recalled, or insurance-flagged, skip the guesswork and go straight to panel replacement or a panel upgrade. If you are not sure, start here, a short diagnostic is cheaper than replacing the wrong thing.
