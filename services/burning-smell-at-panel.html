What a Burning Smell Usually Means

Electrical burning is not the same as a dusty heater smell that fades after a few minutes. Persistent odor at the panel points to heat on a connection that should stay cool.

  • Loose lug or breaker that arcs and heats insulation
  • Overloaded circuit cooking a breaker that will not clear the fault cleanly
  • Failing main or branch breaker with worn contacts
  • Damaged or recalled panel (including Federal Pacific and Zinsco) that needs replacement, not another reset
Do this now

Do not keep flipping breakers. Do not spray cleaners into the panel. If smoke appears, get out and call 911, then Spark Doc at (913) 390-3780.

Smell vs. Sparks

Burning odor without visible sparks still needs a licensed look. If you also see sparks, buzzing, or crackling, use our sparking panel guide and call immediately.

Older Olathe and metro homes with Federal Pacific Stab-Lok or Zinsco panels are a special case. Those brands have well-documented failure modes. See Federal Pacific / Zinsco panels and panel replacement.

Related symptoms

Warm panel cover, breakers that will not stay on, or half the house dark can show up with the same heat fault. We may also check for a partial power outage or a breaker that keeps tripping.

Common Questions

Burning Smell FAQ

Is a burning smell at the breaker panel dangerous?
Yes. It usually means a connection or breaker is overheating. That heat can damage insulation and start a fire inside the panel or wall. Call a licensed electrician instead of waiting to see if the smell goes away.
What should I do if my electrical panel smells like burning plastic?
Stop resetting breakers. Keep people away from the panel. If you see smoke or flames, leave and call 911. If there is odor only, call Spark Doc Electric at (913) 390-3780 and describe what you smell and when it started.
Can a burning smell be dust instead of a real problem?
Sometimes a rarely used heater or motor smells briefly when dust burns off. A smell that comes from the breaker panel, returns, or pairs with heat, trips, or flicker is not dust. Get it checked.
Do I need a full panel replacement if it smells burned?
Not always. A single failing breaker or loose lug can be repaired. If the bus is damaged, the panel is scorched, or the brand is Federal Pacific or Zinsco, replacement is often the right call. We diagnose first and quote honestly.
