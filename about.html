Who We Are

Spark Doc Electric is a local shop based in Olathe. Bobby Wheeler, Owner & Electrician, does the work himself and cares about the next call from a neighbor. When you call, you get Bobby: he shows up himself and tells you straight what the house needs.

Most of our work is residential: panel upgrades and replacement, troubleshooting and repairs, GFCI outlet repair, home EV chargers, and the rest of our services.

How We Work

  • Diagnose before we sell a fix. We find the actual problem first.
  • The price before we start. You'll know the cost before we start. No hidden fees, no surprise bill.
  • Permits and inspection handled. Panel work includes permits and inspection coordination with the city when required.
  • Clean work. Fixtures centered, wiring concealed, and access points patched before we leave.

5-Year Warranty

We back every job with a 5-year warranty. If something isn't right, we come back and make it right.
